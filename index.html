Connection help & privacy

Base URLs such as https://api.example.com/v1 work with Custom endpoint unchecked. Standard full endpoint paths are detected automatically. Check the option only to use a custom URL without adding a path.

Leave the key blank only if your endpoint requires no key. A local model server can use an address such as http://localhost:1234/v1 and its loaded model ID. It must allow this site's origin through CORS; your browser may also ask for permission to connect. Localhost refers to the device running this browser.

Effort applies to moves, chat and Test. Supported levels depend on your model and provider. Extra high and Max can take longer and cost more.

Without move permission, this AI seat waits. Enable the option and Save & apply to continue. In AI vs AI, also press Play. Test and chat can send requests independently.
