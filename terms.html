About this project

This is an independent open-source project. Its code, change history and third-party credits are available in the source repository.

Free play and optional providers

This application does not charge for built-in AI or local play. Hosting, device and network costs are outside the software's control. External AI is optional: its provider may charge your account when you test an endpoint, explicitly allow external turn requests, or send an AI message. You choose that provider and must comply with its terms, regional availability and age requirements.

Fair and lawful use

Use rooms with a trusted opponent. Do not use this software to harass others, send unlawful content, steal credentials, disrupt services, misrepresent identity, gamble or violate others' rights. Rooms carry moves and game controls such as rematch requests; they provide no player text chat or public content feed. AI replies are machine-generated and can be wrong; they are not professional advice.

Data and third-party services

Read the privacy and data notice before connecting an AI provider or room. The project cannot erase a provider's or opponent's records. Third-party services can be unavailable, and peer connections can fail. No uptime, uninterrupted service or victory against another AI is promised.

Invitation rooms use either a Cloudflare Workers relay or the public ntfy.sh service. Each has usage limits. If a relay room disconnects, rejoin with the original invitation while the host keeps the page open. If a Cloudflare room reaches its six-hour session limit, create a new room. A separate Direct room uses public PeerJS signalling and requires a working WebRTC connection.
